How to calibrate white balance across cameras:

  1. Complete stitch calibration first (above).
  2. Place a white or neutral grey card in front of all cameras so each camera can clearly see it.
  3. Use the same lighting you use during actual captures — don't calibrate under different lights.
  4. Click Calibrate WB. All cameras capture simultaneously and colour correction gains are computed relative to Camera 0 (the reference).
  5. Re-calibrate if you change the lighting setup, replace a camera, or notice colour seams in stitched output.

Individual camera WB settings are locked while group calibration is active. Use this section to adjust white balance instead.

Exposure Sync

Experimental — keep all cameras at the same brightness by copying one camera's auto-exposure to the others.

Experimental
What this fixes: some cameras' pictures can flicker brighter/darker between shots because their auto-exposure has nothing fixed in view to measure against — usually the ones aimed at nothing but plain material. This page lets you pick one steady camera as the reference, take a test shot, fine-tune it if needed, then copy that same exposure to the other cameras so all of them stay consistent together.

Nothing changes on the live production line until you press Save in Step 7 and turn the toggle below on. Every step before that is just a preview you can redo as many times as you like.

Feature Status

Unknown

When on, every camera except the reference locks to the last saved calibration the next time it starts running on the line. When off, every camera goes back to its own independent auto-exposure — today's normal behavior. You can only flip this while the line is stopped.

Calibration

1. Pick a reference camera

Choose the camera whose picture already looks the steadiest from run to run — that one keeps adjusting itself automatically. The other cameras will be locked to copy whatever it picks.

2. Watch all three cameras

Just for looking — starting or stopping this doesn't change anything on the cameras. Use it to judge which one looks steadiest before picking a reference in Step 1.

3. Take a test shot

Takes one normal auto-exposed photo on the reference camera and shows you the exposure time and gain it picked. This is the starting point for the next step.

4. Adjust if needed

5. Copy to the other cameras

Writes the number above to the other two cameras right now, so you can check the result in the next step. This is temporary — it's lost if the app restarts before you Save in Step 7.

6. Check that they match

Takes a fresh photo from all three cameras at once so you can compare them side by side. If they still don't look right, go back to Step 4, adjust, and Apply again — repeat as many times as needed.

7. Save

This is the only step that makes it permanent. Saves the reference camera and its exposure/gain so they're remembered even after a restart, and applies them to the other cameras' saved config. Don't forget to also turn the toggle above on — Save alone doesn't enable the feature.

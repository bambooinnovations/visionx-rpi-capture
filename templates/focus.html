Turn the focus ring slowly and watch the overlay. The stream shows:

  • Coloured edge highlights — focus peaking (brighter = sharper edges)
  • Score bar — higher is sharper; bar fills as you improve
  • Arrow indicator — ↑ KEEP TURNING improving · ↓ TURN BACK dropping · ● BEST FOCUS at peak

When the indicator shows BEST FOCUS, lock the focus ring and return to calibration.

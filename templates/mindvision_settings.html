Factory Reset Camera {{ camera_id }}

This will reset all camera parameters to factory defaults and save them to production:

  • Auto-exposure and auto gain on, target brightness 100
  • Analog gain and RGB gains reset to neutral
  • Sharpness, gamma, contrast, and saturation reset
  • Noise filter, dead pixel correction, and image invert off
  • Rotation 0°, mirrors off
  • Monochrome off

Lens distortion and stitch calibration are not affected.
